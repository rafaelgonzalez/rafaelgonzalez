I’ve moved off GitHub. 👋

You can [find me on Codeberg](https://codeberg.org/rafaelgonzalez), but most of my code will be on a self-hosted [Forgejo](https://forgejo.org/) instance anyway, to keep it away from LLM creeps.

The short of it is I've had enough of GitHub’s overall enshittification, degradation of service,[^1][^2] deal-breaking bugs,[^3] security issues[^4][^5] and having to depend on corporations like ~~Microsoft~~ Microslop[^6][^7] who actively make software a liability.[^8][^9][^10] There were already many other reasons to move on: the [Give Up GitHub](https://sfconservancy.org/GiveUpGitHub/) initiative covers those at length.

The longer version is a bit of a rant.

I'm tired of the evident lack of care for consent,[^11][^12][^13][^14] copyright[^15][^16][^17][^18][^19][^20] environmental harms[^21][^22][^23][^24][^25][^26] and labour rights[^27] that tech CEOs like Microslop's Satya Nadella exhibit when training, deploying and shoving their monstrous LLMs down our throats.[^28][^29][^30]

They have helped unleash a flood of GenAI slop[^31] that has been detrimental to the arts,[^32] open-source,[^33][^34][^35][^36][^37][^38] the open web,[^39] research,[^40][^41] our language,[^42] thoughts[^43] and our already fragile sense of shared reality.[^44]

The tech is fundamentally unserious,[^45] inaccurate,[^46][^47][^48][^49] insecure,[^50][^51] unpopular,[^52][^53][^54][^55] unprofitable[^56][^57][^58][^59][^60] and economically ruinous.[^61] It is definitely not intelligent nor conscious nor sentient. %% add sources %% It is a con[^62] and a cognitive trap.[^63] It is causing hardware shortages and has made costs skyrocket.[^64][^65][^66]

To its users it inflicts cognitive decline,[^67][^68][^69][^70] deskilling,[^71][^72][^73] addiction,[^74] psychosis[^75][^76] and self-harm.[^77][^78][^79] It has brought stress,[^80] burnout[^81] and despair[^82] to workers.

CEOs have wilfully ignored these harms and threatened their workforce to use-AI-or-else[^83] for imaginary productivity increases,[^84] then used AI as an excuse anyway to enact mass layoffs,[^85][^86][^87][^88][^89][^90] sometimes backtracking[^91][^92] or rehiring[^93] after facing backlash or realising a chatbot is not replacement for labour.

Above all, I am exhausted from bloviating[^94][^95][^96] Silicon-Valley thought-leader types trying to convince us a technology they deeply misunderstand[^97] is inevitable. I'm sick of their rotten ideologies[^98] and their dystopian visions of the future[^99][^100][^101][^102] they want to force on us. 

If this sentiment resonates with you and – in the context of GitHub – you would like to reclaim some of your digital sovereignty, then I’d encourage you to move off if you can. There are several alternatives:

- [Codeberg](https://codeberg.org/), SaaS code forge, operated by a non-profit, funded through donations. It runs on Forgejo.
- [Forgejo](https://forgejo.org/), self-hostable, open-source code forge software. Maintained by the Codeberg non-profit. It is a fork of Gitea.
- [SourceHut](https://sourcehut.org/), SaaS and self-hostable open-source code forge software. Explicitly no AI, tracking or ads.

I would actively discourage you from using Gitea[^103] or GitLab.[^104][^105]

I will still engage with GitHub where absolutely necessary, like reporting bugs on projects still hosted on the platform. But I won’t be hosting my own code there anymore. My hope is over time the open-source community moves off.

Beyond GitHub, my advice would be to seek decentralised, federated software (not platforms!) that do what you need, e.g [Forgejo](https://forgejo.org/), [Matrix](https://matrix.org/), [OpenCloud](https://opencloud.eu/), [Mastodon](https://joinmastodon.org/about), etc. and support those you find really helpful. I'd also encourage you helping organisations who have a positive impact on the open web, like Wikipedia, through [donations](https://donate.wikimedia.org) or [contributions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Introduction).

As for the scourge of GenAI itself, if you work with tech check out [Open Slopware](https://codeberg.org/small-hack/open-slopware) to avoid open-source software tainted by LLM slop. Otherwise, tune into folks like [Karen Hao](https://karendhao.com), Ed Zitron's [newsletter](https://www.wheresyoured.at/) and [podcast](https://www.youtube.com/@BetterOfflinePod), [Adam Becker](https://freelanceastrophysicist.com/)'s [book](https://bookshop.org/p/books/more-everything-forever-ai-overlords-space-empires-and-silicon-valley-s-crusade-to-control-the-fate-of-humanity-adam-becker) and [podcast](https://www.dreamingagainstthemachine.com/), and [Brian Merchant's newsletter](https://www.bloodinthemachine.com/). They have helped me sift through the hype and bullshit, kept me sane and helped me educate myself on this topic. Hopefully they will help you, too. And if you have been asked to embrace AI, why not become _actually_ informed on the subject by [reading lots of books](https://www.bloodinthemachine.com/p/the-best-books-films-and-tv-that) about it?

Good luck out there. ✌️
